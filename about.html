About

Robots should understand the people who run them.

Seventh Axis is a software company in Kuala Lumpur. We make robot arms as easy to direct as a new colleague on the line.

Why we started

Malaysian factories are short of hands. Wages and levies are rising, and robot arms have never been cheaper. Yet most cells still sit on one job for years, because changing what a robot does takes a specialist, a teach pendant and days of downtime.

A robot arm moves on six axes. We think the seventh is understanding: an arm that takes instructions in the language your team already speaks, English, Bahasa Malaysia, Chinese or Manglish, and shows you exactly what it will do before it does anything.

We don't sell robots. We build the software that makes the one you buy worth more, and we build it here, for Malaysian lines first.
