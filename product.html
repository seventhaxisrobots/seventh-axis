Capabilities

Everything between a sentence and a safe move.

Natural-language interface

English, Bahasa Malaysia, 中文 and Manglish, typed or spoken. Describe tasks, goals or whole workflows.

Digital twin

A live 3D model of your cell. Every plan runs here first, with real kinematics, physics and cycle times.

Motion planning and checks

Inverse kinematics, reach, clearance, payload and fragile-item rules, with speed limits written into the program.

Signed approvals

A named person approves the exact program. The signature covers one run on one robot, and expires.

Teach your own cell

Describe your conveyors, pallets, fixtures and bins once. Kata plans against your layout, not a demo.

Integrations

A bridge for Universal Robots controllers over RTDE and the Dashboard Server, plus URScript you can review.
