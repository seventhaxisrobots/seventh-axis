Cookie policy

What we store in your browser, and why. The short version: only what the site needs, plus optional analytics if you say yes.

Effective 30 September 2026

What we use

NameTypePurposeKept for
sa_consent Local storage, strictly necessary Remembers your cookie choice so we don't ask on every page. Until you clear your browser storage
sa_intro, sa_film Session storage, functional Remembers that the intro and launch film have played, so they don't replay on every visit to the home page. Until you close the tab
Vercel Web Analytics Optional, only if you accept analytics Counts page views so we can see which pages are useful. It sets no cookies and doesn't identify you or follow you to other sites. Aggregated statistics only

What we don't use

  • No advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking.
  • No social media pixels.
  • No third-party fonts: our typefaces are served from our own domain.

The Studio microphone

If you press the microphone button in the Studio, your browser asks for permission and uses its own speech service to transcribe what you say. That service is run by your browser's maker under their privacy terms, not by us.
