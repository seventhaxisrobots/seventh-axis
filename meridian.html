Mission control as a service

Launch is getting cheaper, so more teams can put a satellite in orbit than can afford to build and staff a mission-control centre. Meridian gives you one: your fleet, your maneuvers, your telemetry and your alerts in one place, with a second named operator approving every burn.

Capabilities

Fleet registry

Every spacecraft in one list, with its orbit kept current from public tracking data, its ground stations and its next contact windows.

Maneuver planning

Orbit raises and lowers, plane changes and phasing, with Δv, propellant and timing worked out. Your fleet can be screened against the catalogue for close approaches.

Telemetry pipeline

Send data as JSON or CSV. Meridian stores it, checks every value against limits, raises alerts and charts it.

One ops view

Live ground tracks, passes, plans, telemetry and alerts on one screen, so the operator on shift sees the whole fleet at once.

How a maneuver gets approved

Meridian plans the burn. A second named operator approves it. Every decision is logged.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Meridian works out the burn: Δv, propellant, timing and the resulting orbit.

  2. 2

    Check

    Meridian checks the burn against the propellant on board, and you can screen the orbit for close approaches before deciding.

  3. 3

    Second operator approves

    A different named operator from the one who raised the plan reviews it and approves or rejects it.

  4. 4

    Logged

    Who planned, who approved and when are recorded for audit and hand-over.
