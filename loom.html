Codon optimisation you can watch

A gene that expresses well in one host can fail in another, and the lab usually finds out weeks later, after the synthesis order and the first failed induction. Loom optimises the sequence for your host and then runs the ribosomes along it, so the places translation stalls are something you can see rather than infer from an average.

Why a score is not enough

Codon adaptation scores are averages over a whole sequence. Two designs can score the same and express differently, because ribosomes do not move independently: one slow codon holds up everything behind it, and the queue is where the yield goes. Loom simulates that traffic directly, so the number comes with a picture of what produced it.

Capabilities

Optimise for your host

E. coli, S. cerevisiae, CHO and Pichia, with usage counted from each organism's own reference genome. Maximise the adaptation, or harmonise against the source host to keep the slow patches a native gene may depend on.

Constraints that hold

Restriction sites you name are removed, GC is held in a window, homopolymers and repeats are broken, and the first codons stay deliberately slow. Every repair is listed in plain language, including the ones that could not be made.

Ribosome traffic

A live simulation of ribosomes initiating, elongating, queueing and colliding along your mRNA, with per-codon density, collisions and protein flux. Change one codon and watch the jam clear.

Nothing leaves unrecorded

A sequence cannot be exported for synthesis without a deliberate, recorded act. The gate writes an audit record; the authoritative screen stays the one your synthesis provider runs.

From sequence to a decision

Four steps, and you can stop at any of them.

  1. 1

    Paste the protein

    A plain sequence or a FASTA record, and the host you intend to express in.

  2. 2

    Optimise

    Loom back-translates, repairs the constraints and reports CAI, tAI, GC, rare-codon runs and repeats against a naive baseline.

  3. 3

    Run the traffic

    Watch where ribosomes pile up. Click a codon, try a synonym, run it again and compare.

  4. 4

    Export

    Take the FASTA to your synthesis provider, with a record of what was screened and when.

What Loom does not claim

The expression score is a transparent heuristic over sequence features, not a model fitted to expression data, and its coefficients are printed next to the number rather than hidden behind it. The 5′ figure is a structure proxy, not a folding energy. tRNA weights are estimated from gene copy number, and for CHO and Pichia they are derived from codon usage, which the interface says plainly. None of it replaces an expression trial. A tool that states its limits is worth more than one that implies it has none.
