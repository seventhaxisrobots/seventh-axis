Cost questions

What does the robot cell itself cost?

A collaborative palletising or machine-tending cell with arm, gripper, guarding and installation typically costs RM120,000 to RM250,000 from an integrator. Kata doesn't sell or mark up hardware.

Does the Smart Automation Grant help?

It's a matching grant for Malaysian manufacturers that automate. Robot cells and their integration are typical eligible costs; how much it covers depends on approval.
