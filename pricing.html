Pricing

Pay for the software, not a programmer.

Seventh Axis is software for the robot arms you buy from your integrator. Start with a free pilot on one cell.

Prices exclude SST. The robot arm, gripper and installation come from your integrator and aren't included. The Smart Automation Grant can match part of eligible automation costs.
