Maritime domain awareness for Malaysian waters

Heimdall correlates live AIS with satellite SAR detections across the Malaysian EEZ to surface dark targets, deliberate AIS disabling and ship-to-ship encounters. Built for IUU fishing enforcement and narcotics interdiction, with every case dispositioned by a named analyst.

Capabilities

Dark-target correlation

Sentinel-1 SAR detections correlated against AIS. Uncorrelated targets are clustered by cell and pass, with fishing / non-fishing classification.

AIS disabling

Transmission gaps flagged within the hour where receiver coverage is confirmed, with last track and dead-reckoned position.

Ship-to-ship encounters

Rendezvous at sea with both vessels' identity, flag state, type, duration, separation and distance offshore. Carriers and bunkers marked as probable transfers.

Case management

A single queue of cases with evidence. Analysts approve or dismiss with a note; every disposition is logged with identity and time.

Mission sets

IUU fishing

Detection of unlicensed and foreign fishing activity inside the EEZ.

  • Clusters of uncorrelated SAR targets classified as likely fishing
  • Fishing-to-carrier encounters indicating at-sea transshipment
  • AIS disabling by fishing vessels inside the EEZ
  • Flag state and vessel type on every correlated contact

Narcotics interdiction

Cueing for patrol and interdiction assets against at-sea smuggling patterns.

  • Deliberate AIS disabling with confirmed coverage
  • Ship-to-ship encounters away from port and anchorages
  • Non-fishing dark targets in coastal approaches
  • Fishing traffic filtered out to reduce analyst load

Case view

Each case carries its evidence: detections or track, position and range from the nearest town, time of pass or last transmission, correlated traffic in the area and the rule that raised it. Analysts disposition from the same view.

Workflow

  1. 1

    Detect

    SAR passes and the live AIS picture over the full EEZ.

  2. 2

    Correlate

    Detections matched to transmitting vessels; residuals and anomalies isolated.

  3. 3

    Raise

    Dark-target clusters, AIS disabling and encounters opened as cases with evidence.

  4. 4

    Disposition

    A named analyst approves or dismisses. Decisions are logged for audit and hand-off.

Specification

Operational

  • Coverage: Malaysian EEZ, Strait of Malacca to east of Sabah
  • AIS: terrestrial, live, 48-hour track history
  • SAR: Sentinel-1, 30-day window, published about 5 days after pass
  • AIS-disabling cases raised within 60 minutes
  • Satellite and daily imagery base layers
  • Access control by approved account; full disposition log

In development
