01 · Language

Speaks the line's language.

Your supervisors shouldn't need a robotics course. Say the task the way you'd say it to a new hire, in any language your floor speaks.

  • English Pick the red boxes off the conveyor and stack them three high on pallet A.
  • BM Ambil kotak merah dari konveyor, susun tiga tingkat atas palet A.
  • 中文 把传送带上的红色箱子搬到A号托盘，叠三层。
  • Manglish Boss, red boxes go pallet A, three layer can?

02 · Why now

Robots got cheaper. Programming them didn't.

Arms are affordable and grants cover most of the cost. The bottleneck is the specialist who has to program every new task.

  • RM1,700

    National minimum wage, compulsory for every employer since 1 August 2025.

  • RM1,850

    Yearly levy per foreign manufacturing worker in the Peninsula, with a multi-tier levy on the way.

    FMT (opens in a new tab)

  • 70%

    Of automation costs the Smart Automation Grant can match, up to RM1 million per company.

03 · How it works

Say it. See it. Ship it.

  1. You

    Load the CNC, take finished parts to the tray.

    01 Say it

    Type or speak. Seventh Axis reads the cell, finds the parts and asks when something is unclear.

  2. Digital twin · Reach OK

    02 See it

    Every plan runs in a digital twin first. Reach, collisions and cycle time are checked before anything moves.

  3. def load_cnc():
  movej(home, a=1.2, v=0.8)
  movel(p[0.42,-0.30,0.18,0,3.14,0])
  set_digital_out(0, True)
end

    03 Ship it

    Approve it and export controller code. URScript for Universal Robots today, more controllers on the roadmap.

04 · Use cases

Built for Malaysian lines.

High mix, short runs, tasks that change every week. The work robots were too hard to reprogram for.

  • E&E and appliances

    Tray loading, screwdriving and test-station tending.

  • Palletising

    Mixed-SKU pallets from a sentence, not a pattern editor.

  • Small-batch & labs

    Change the task before lunch, without an integrator visit.

  • Machine tending

    CNC, moulding and presses, loaded around the clock.

05 · Safety

Nothing moves until a human says so.

  1. 01Every program runs in the digital twin first.
  2. 02Speed and zone limits you set are written into the exported code.
  3. 03An operator approves each change, and every change is logged.
