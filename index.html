How Kata works

From a sentence to a running cell, in four checked steps.

  1. 01

    Say it

    "Susun 12 kotak di palet 2." Kata reads the cell, the parts and the words your team already uses.

    kata › stack 12 boxes on pallet 2
  2. 02

    Check it

    The plan runs in a digital twin of your cell first: reach, clearance, payload and cycle time.

    reach ✓ collisions ✓ payload ✓
  3. 03

    Approve it

    A supervisor signs the exact program. The signature covers one run, on one robot, for a short time.

    approved · Aisyah · 1 run · 5 min
  4. 04

    Run it

    The bridge next to your controller verifies the signature, then runs the job and streams it back.

    bridge › running on UR10e
